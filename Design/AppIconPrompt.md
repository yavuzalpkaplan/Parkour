# Parkour app icon

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iOS game app icon, one square full-bleed opaque 1024x1024 image for a game named Parkour. Primary request: a polished bold icon based on the actual game: a bright orange square cube runner with one small white square eye near its upper-right front face, leaping toward the right over a gap between dark rooftop ledges with thin electric cyan and pink neon rims. The orange cube is the dominant large centered silhouette, slightly tilted mid-jump, occupying roughly 50 percent of the canvas. Clean contemporary 2D arcade illustration with crisp geometric forms, restrained soft highlights, deep midnight navy / purple background, subtle distant rooftop silhouettes; strong orange versus cyan contrast. One clear jumping gesture, minimal background detail, readable at 60px. Composition keeps the cube and rooftop edges within the central 80 percent for iOS corner masking. No text, no letters, no title, no watermark, no mockup phone, no surrounding margin, no pre-rounded corners, no transparent pixels. Fill the square entirely. Avoid photorealism, complex city detail, glossy toy rendering, extra characters, limbs, and UI controls.
